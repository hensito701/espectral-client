Minecraft Espectral (MinecraftEspectral-Regular.ttf, MinecraftEspectral-Bold.ttf)

Pixel font built by espectral-web/scripts/build-mc-font.py from the glyph
atlases of the Minecraft client's `default` font (ascii, accented,
nonlatin_european). The glyph artwork belongs to Mojang Studios; it is the
same font espectral.uhce.top serves as minecraft-espectral-*.woff2, converted
to TTF for the native launcher.
